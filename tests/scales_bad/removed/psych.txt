# SLOOP scales: PSYCH set (index 52..64, then the RAST family at 65..67)
# Microtonal psych: makam and maqam scales as King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard play them on
# quarter-tone-fretted guitars, in equal quarter tones (24-TET cents, so every offset is 0).
# Sources: The Pop Descriptivist, "The Microtonal Scales of King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard
#   (a Primer and Compendium)", 2024: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TiGI-5i2wZ8 and slides at
#   https://sites.google.com/site/thepopdescriptivist/home/the-microtonal-scales-of-king-gizzard-and-the-lizard-wizard
#   maqamworld.com (Ushaq Masri, Hijaz, Rast, Mahur); Wikipedia, "Acemli Rast"; Touma, The Music of the Arabs;
#   Bor (ed.), The Raga Guide (Bhairav); Farhat, The Dastgah Concept in Persian Music (Chahargah, Homayun).
#
# Five families, 4 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3, built the way the slides analyse KGLW's scales: tetrachord symmetry.
# Each is a 12-TET frame of five notes plus a pair of notes that move, one in each tetrachord (the 2nd
# and 6th, or the 3rd and 7th). The core, listed first, puts both on the same rung; the others are variations.
#   HUSEYNI (4)   frame 1 b3 4 5 b7    slot 2: neutral (150) | major (200)   slot 6: minor | neutral | major
#   HIJAZ (3)     frame 1 b2 4 5 b6    slot 3: neutral (350) | major (400)   slot 7: b7 | neutral (1050) | maj 7
#   SUBMINOR (3)  frame 1 b3 4 5 b7    slot 2: subminor (50) | minor | neutral   slot 6: subminor (750) | minor
#   BHAIRAV (3)   frame 1 3 4 5 7      slot 2: low (50) | neutral (150)   slot 6: low (750) | minor | neutral (850)
#   RAST (3)      frame 1 2 4 5 6      slot 3: neutral (350)               slot 7: minor | neutral (1050) | major
# HUSEYNI and SUBMINOR share a frame: together the 2nd runs 50-200 c and the 6th 750-900 c.
# BHAIRAV's 2nd and 6th run the same ladder on the major frame: low (50/750) to neutral (150/850).
# Neutral = halfway between minor and major (150, 350, 850, 1050 c); subminor = a quarter tone
# above the note below (50 c above the root, 750 c = 50 c above the 5th).
# home = the traditional tonic (Turkish for Huseyni / Ussak / Neva / Hicazkar, Arabic for
# Ushaq Masri / Hijaz / Rast / Mahur; Turkish Rast is on G); the SUBMINOR family has no tradition and uses KGLW's roots (G; See Me F#);
# BHAIRAV has Sa = C.

# ---- HUSEYNI family: lower jins Bayati (or Nahawand), upper jins on the 5th varies ----
scale P.HUS "Huseyni" home A    # Bayati + Bayati on 5. KGLW: Sleep Drifter, Rattlesnake, Honey (F#)
  up   0. 150. 300. 500. 700. 850. 1000.

scale P.MSR "UshMasri" home D   # Nahawand + Bayati on 5. KGLW: Anoxia (B), Flying Microtonal Banana zurna (F#)
  up   0. 200. 300. 500. 700. 850. 1000.

scale P.NEV "Neva" home A       # Bayati + Nahawand on 5 (Turkish Neva ends on dugah, like Huseyni). KGLW: Doom City bass riff, Oddlife vocals (F#)
  up   0. 150. 300. 500. 700. 900. 1000.

scale P.USK "Ussak" home A      # Bayati + Kurd on 5. KGLW: Pleura, Minimum Brain Size guitar (C#)
  up   0. 150. 300. 500. 700. 800. 1000.

# ---- HIJAZ family: the core narrows both augmented 2nds to neutral; Hicazkar is its 12-TET twin ----
scale P.NHK "NeutHkar" home G   # core: neutral 3rd + neutral 7th (2-5-3 | 2-5-3). KGLW: Static Electricity, East West Link (G)
  up   0. 100. 350. 500. 700. 800. 1050.

scale P.HKR "Hicazkar" home G   # Hijaz + Hijaz on 5 (double harmonic, equal-tempered)
  up   0. 100. 400. 500. 700. 800. 1100.

scale P.HJZ "Hijaz" home D      # Hijaz + Nahawand on 5 (Phrygian dominant, equal-tempered)
  up   0. 100. 400. 500. 700. 800. 1000.

# ---- SUBMINOR family: Phrygian with the 2nd and 6th squeezed toward the note below ----
scale P.SUB "SubPhryg" home G   # subminor 2nd + subminor 6th. KGLW: Open Water, Intrasport (G), Ataraxia (A, D)
  up   0. 50. 300. 500. 700. 750. 1000.

scale P.SU6 "SubPhr6" home G    # minor 2nd, subminor 6th: plain Phrygian with only the 6th squeezed
  up   0. 100. 300. 500. 700. 750. 1000.

scale P.SEE "SeeMe" home Gb     # neutral 2nd + subminor 6th. KGLW: See Me (F#; written Gb: "#" starts a comment here)
  up   0. 150. 300. 500. 700. 750. 1000.

# ---- BHAIRAV family: the frame of raga Bhairav (S G m P N), whose re and dha are its andolan notes, sung
# low. The pair moves together as in the slides' other families: low (50 / 750 c) is Bhairav leaning low,
# stylised a quarter tone below komal; minor (100 / 800) is Bhairav itself (= Hicazkar, in the HIJAZ family);
# neutral (150 / 850) is Persian dastgah Chahargah, whose 2nd and 6th are koron. Sa = the root; home C.
scale P.BHL "LowBhair" home C   # core: low re + low dha (1-7-2 | 1-7-2). KGLW: If Not Now, Then When? (G, slide 51)
  up   0. 50. 400. 500. 700. 750. 1100.

scale P.CHG "Chahrgah" home C   # Chahargah: neutral re + neutral dha (3-5-2 | 3-5-2), the symmetric neutral twin
  up   0. 150. 400. 500. 700. 850. 1100.

scale P.HMY "Homayun" home C    # Homayun: Chahargah's lower half + Kurd on 5 (3-5-2 | 2-4-4). KGLW: Ontology (C#, slide 57)
  up   0. 150. 400. 500. 700. 800. 1000.

# ---- RAST family: the neutral 3rd of jins Rast, with the 7th as the moving partner. KGLW: Honey (F# Rast,
# "a good fit" for its trilled neutral 3rd and 7th). The 12-TET twin, Mixolydian, is in WEST.
# Appended after BHAIRAV (indices 65..67): PSYCH's first 13 indices stay where PR #4 put them.
scale P.RST "Rast" home C       # core: Rast + Rast on 5 (4-3-3 | 4-3-3), Arabic Rast ascending. KGLW: Honey (F#)
  up   0. 200. 350. 500. 700. 900. 1050.

scale P.ACR "AcemRast" home G   # Acemli Rast: Rast + Buselik on 5 (minor 7th, acem); Arabic Rast descending. KGLW: Honey (F#)
  up   0. 200. 350. 500. 700. 900. 1000.

scale P.MHR "Mahur" home C      # Mahur: Rast + Ajam on 5 (major 7th)
  up   0. 200. 350. 500. 700. 900. 1100.
